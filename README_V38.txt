SPORTIVO V38 — COACH TRAINING PLAN MANAGEMENT

- Coach Dashboard now shows a summary of the coach's next scheduled training sessions.
- Training Requests summary removes the Rejected count.
- Added Coach > Training Plans module.
- Coach can set/edit a plan per trainee and training group.
- Training plan includes phase, goal, weekly focus, sessions/week, review date, status and coach notes.
- Saving a plan notifies the trainee.
- The plan automatically appears in Trainee > My Training for the corresponding sport/group.
- HTML, CSS and JavaScript remain separated.
