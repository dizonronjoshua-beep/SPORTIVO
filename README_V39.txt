SPORTIVO V39 — DETAILED COACH TRAINING PLANS

- Coach Training Plans now contains a detailed five-section training plan form.
- Plan Overview: phase, status, level, program duration, sessions/week, session duration, start date, review date.
- Development Focus: main goal, weekly focus, technical, tactical, physical, and mental priorities.
- Session Structure: warm-up, main skill drills, application drills, conditioning, and cool-down.
- Milestones & Evaluation: short-term milestones, long-term milestones, evaluation criteria, home practice.
- Requirements: equipment, safety/medical considerations, and coach instructions.
- Saved plan automatically reflects in Trainee > My Training under the selected sport.
- Existing navigation and role flow remain unchanged.
