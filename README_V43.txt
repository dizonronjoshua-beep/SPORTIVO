SPORTIVO V43 — RESPONSIBILITY TRANSFER

ADMIN PORTAL
- Removed Trainee Management.
- Removed Training Groups.
- Removed Attendance.
- Admin keeps user accounts, coach accounts, master schedule, courts, bookings, sessions, progress monitoring, announcements, appeals, reports, logs, settings, and profile.

COACH PORTAL
- Training Groups is now coach-managed. Coaches can create/edit their own groups and manage trainee membership.
- My Trainees is renamed Trainee Management. Coaches can review their assigned trainees and move them between their own groups.
- Attendance Recording remains under Coach because attendance is recorded by the coach handling the session.
